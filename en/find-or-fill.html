How to play

1FIND

Spot the number your rival picked in the shuffled grid. A wrong tap locks you for half a second.

2FILL

While your rival searches, tap the boxes on your own sheet. Filled boxes stay filled.

3SWAP

Roles swap when the number is found or after 20 seconds.

4WIN

First to fill all 100 boxes wins the match. Rematch with one tap.

Highlights

  • Pick a 6x6, 8x8 or 10x10 grid and get matched instantly
  • Private duels with a 6-character room code
  • Short, intense matches that last a few minutes
  • Sign in with Google or Apple, or play as a guest
  • Lost connection? The match pauses and resumes
  • No ads during matches
