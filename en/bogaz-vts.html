How to play

1COMMAND

Tap a ship and order FULL AHEAD, SLOW or STOP. Tankers are heavy and stop late: decide early.

2FOLLOW THE RULES

Two large ships never meet in a bend, nobody stops under a bridge. Switch to one-way traffic if needed.

3HANDLE EMERGENCIES

Tugs for breakdowns, the Coast Guard for man overboard, patrols for pirates, pilots for tankers.

4RANK UP

Your score becomes XP; higher ranks unlock the Dardanelles, Suez, Panama and more.

Highlights

  • 7 straits: Bosphorus, Dardanelles, Gibraltar, Suez, Hormuz, Panama, Bab-el-Mandeb
  • Real rules: no meeting in bends, mandatory pilots
  • Story missions: the Cross-Continental Swim, the 18 March ceremony and more
  • Rise from Trainee to VTS Chief
  • Daily missions and a weekly leaderboard
  • Night, fog and storms; plays offline
