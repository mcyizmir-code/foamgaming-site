How to play

1AIM

Press, pull back and release. The further you pull, the stronger the shot.

2SMASH

Topple wooden towers, break stone, blow up barrels. Soldiers who fall are out too.

3SURVIVE

Archers and gunners fire back. Silence the gunner with the red warning first.

4UPGRADE

Spend gold on upgrades, unlock new cannons and finish daily quests.

Highlights

  • 100 castles across 5 worlds, a boss gunner every 10 castles
  • Armored archers, ballistas, standard-bearers and masons
  • 5 cannons: Field Cannon, Long Barrel, Twin Barrel, Mortar, Royal Cannon
  • Upgrade power, armor, reload, blast and ammo
  • Daily reward, daily quests and a new Castle of the Day
  • Plays offline too
